Proofs
------

P1. Reset clears request, bank-enqueue, page-transfer, and completion state.
P2. Port readiness exactly reflects the one-entry translation stage.
P3. Accepted requests retain address, operation, data, strobes, and thread.
P4. Page-message inputs are always accepted and page responses follow capacity.
P5. A stalled page request remains asserted.
P6. Transfer completion and accepted page requests update completion state.
P7. Bank response ownership cannot be consumed without selection.

Formal uses a parameter-equivalent reduced geometry; full geometry is exercised
by `verification/uvm/memory`.
