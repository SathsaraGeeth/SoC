Proofs
------

Reset
P1. Reset or synchronous clear returns the level, pointers, and dequeue state to empty.

Status
P2. The empty flag is asserted exactly when the level is zero.
P3. The full flag is asserted exactly when the level equals depth.
P4. The level never exceeds the configured depth.
P5. Enqueue and dequeue ready agree with full and empty status.

Protocol
P6. An enqueue without a dequeue increments the level.
P7. A dequeue without an enqueue decrements the level.
P8. Simultaneous enqueue/dequeue or no operation preserves the level.
P9. The buffer cannot accept a dequeue while empty or an enqueue while full.

Data
P10. An arbitrarily selected accepted enqueue is returned only after every older entry and with unchanged data.

Cover
-----------

C1. Cover reset release in the empty state.
C2. Cover an accepted enqueue.
C3. Cover reaching the full state.
C4. Cover simultaneous enqueue and dequeue.
C5. Cover write-pointer wraparound.
C6. Cover synchronous clear from a nonempty state.
