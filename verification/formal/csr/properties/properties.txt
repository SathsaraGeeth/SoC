Proofs
------

P1. Reset boots every thread in machine mode with interrupts disabled.
P2. Thread ID, MIP, SIP, and delegation read views are exact.
P3. Timer interrupt pending follows delegation, enable, and privilege state.
P4. Machine and supervisor traps select the requested privilege.
P5. MRET and SRET restore saved privilege state.

Cover
-----

C1. CSR writes, traps, returns, and a pending timer interrupt are reachable.
