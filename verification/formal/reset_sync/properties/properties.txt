Proofs
------

Reset
P1. An asynchronous reset clears every synchronizer stage and the output.
P2. The reset output always equals the final synchronizer stage.

Protocol
P3. Reset release advances exactly one pipeline stage per clock.
P4. Once released, reset remains released until asynchronous reset assertion.

Cover
-----

C1. Cover a complete reset release.
C2. Cover asynchronous reset reassertion after release.
