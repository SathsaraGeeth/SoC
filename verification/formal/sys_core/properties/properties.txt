Proofs
------
P1. Reset clears command, ownership, MMIO, interrupt, and counter state.
P2. MMIO readiness obeys response capacity and owner-only access.
P3. Accepted requests create a response that remains stable under backpressure.
P4. Command words shift in the documented order and wrap after four words.
P5. Delegation captures the command TID; release clears ownership.
P6. Ownership and owner identity remain stable between ownership commands.

Child datapaths use contracts and are covered by their separate formal/UVM suites.
